125
God Pipol Dey Save
David Song
1 Pipol wey trust God dey sikure like Mount Zion;
nobody go fit bend dem and dem go dey forever.
2 Just as mountin gada round Jerusalem,
na so too God dey round en pipol,
both naw and forever.
3 Wiked pipol nor go rule pipol wey dey fear and obey God,
if not, doz wey dey obey God fit start to do bad tins too.
4 My God, make Yu do good to pipol wey dey good;
doz wey dia heart and mind dey with Yu,
5 but make Yu ponish pipol wey dey do bad tins.
Ponish dem with all di oda wiked pipol.
Make peace always dey Israel land!
