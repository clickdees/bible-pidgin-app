14
As Man Wiked Rish
For Di Kwayamasta: David Song
1 Na only fools dey tok for dia heart sey, “God nor dey.”
Dem nor good at-all and dia ways bad well-well.
No one among dem dey do wetin good!
2 God dey look evribody from heaven;
E dey look to si if anybody really get sense and dey find am.
3 But e nor dey, bikos evribody don turn from am; evribody don spoil finish.
Nobody dey do good again.
Yes! E nor get anybody at-all.
4 Doz wey dey do bad nor go ever learn?
Dem dey chop my pipol like bread and dem nor dey pray to God.
5 So fear go katch dem,
bikos God dey with di pipol wey dey obey am.
6 Even doh wiked pipol dey fight poor pipol,
God go guide and protet dem.
7 E go from Zion kon save Israel pipol!
So wen God don save en pipol, Jakob shidren go shaut with joy
and Israel pipol go happy well-well.
