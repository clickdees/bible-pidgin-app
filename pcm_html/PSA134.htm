134
Make Wi Praiz God
David Song
1 All of una wey bi God savant and doz wey dey serve for God temple for nite,
make una praiz God.
2 Make una raiz una hand for di temple
kon praiz God!
3 Make God wey kreate heaven
and eart bless una from Zion!
