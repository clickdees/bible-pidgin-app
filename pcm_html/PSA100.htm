100
Praiz Song
1 Make evritin wey dey dis eart,
shaut with joy to God!
2 Make una happy as una dey woship God.
Make una sing with joy as una dey kom meet am.
3 Make una know sey na di Oga God bi God!
Na-im make us and wi bi en pipol.
Wi bi en pipol, di sheep wey dey en field.
4 So make una sing tanksgivin song as una dey enter en present!
Make una enter en temple with tanksgivin.
Make una tank and praiz en name.
5 Bikos God dey good, en love wey nor dey fail dey forever
and E dey good from one generashon go rish anoda.
