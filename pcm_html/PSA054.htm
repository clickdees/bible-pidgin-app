54
Prayer For Proteshon From Enemies
David Song For Di Kwayamasta Wen Zif Pipol Go Ripot Am
1 My God, make Yu kom with yor great pawa kon save mi!
Make Yu defend mi with yor strent.
2 My God, make Yu listin to my prayers.
Make Yu listin to mi as I dey beg.
3 Bikos strenjas dey attack mi;
wiked pipol dey try to kill mi and dem nor kare about God.
4 But na God dey help mi.
Na God dey keep my life!
5 Make evry wiked plan wey my enemies dey plan,
turn back go meet dem.
6 I go just give Yu sakrifice on my own!
My Oga God, I go praiz yor name, bikos e dey good and
7 Yu save mi from my trobols
kon help mi defeat my enemies.
