126
Prayer To Diliver
David Song
1 Wen God take wi en pipol go back to Zion from Babilon,
E bi like sey wi dey dream!
2 Wi bigin laf kon dey sing for joy.
So di oda nashons kon sey,
“Di Oga God don do good tin for dis pipol o-o.”
3 Yes, God don do betta tin for us,
so awa joy don plenty!
4 Oga God, just as wota dey make dry land fresh,
na so too Yu go do for us wen Yu give us awa propaty back.
5 Pipol wey sofa plant dia seed,
go harvest wetin dem plant with happiness.
6 Dem dey kry as dem dey go plant dia seed,
but dem go dey sing with joy as dem dey kom back with wetin dem harvest.
