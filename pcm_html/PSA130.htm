130
Prayer For Help
Solomon Song
1 My God, I dey kry kom meet Yu
with pain for my heart.
2 My Oga God, make Yu listin
and hear mi as I dey kry for mesi!
3 If to sey Yu, my God won kount
all di sins wey pesin kommit,
den na who go fit stand before Yu?
4 But Yu always dey ready to forgive so dat dem go honor Yu.
5 So, I put my hope for God hand,
I trust am with my whole life
and I dey wait for en word wey nor dey fail.
6 Di way I dey find God,
sirious pass as gateman dey wait make day break.
Yes o-o, e sirious pass as gateman dey do for morning.
7 So Israel pipol, make una put una hope for God hand,
bikos God dey show en love
and E always dey ready to save en pipol.
8 E go diliver Israel pipol from di wahala wey dem dey face,
bikos of dia sins.
